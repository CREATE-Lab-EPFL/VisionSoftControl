The distributions in Table~1 and Table~S6 are skewed by a small number of samples. Ten of the 500 test samples (2\%) have an orientation error above $30^\circ$ at one or more tips. Nine of them form three groups of three consecutive samples, i.e., three commanded configurations each recorded three times, and their motion-capture orientation labels imply section bending angles of $87^\circ$--$174^\circ$, far beyond the sampled range (95th percentile $\approx 30^\circ$--$34^\circ$); this is consistent with orientation flips of the tracked rigid bodies rather than with errors of the network. Excluding these 10 samples, the relative position error of Section~II is $9.22 \pm 4.68$~mm (instead of $11.92 \pm 25.10$~mm) and its orientation error $5.97^\circ \pm 3.03^\circ$, and the orientation error of Section~I is $5.10^\circ \pm 2.70^\circ$ (instead of $6.78^\circ \pm 15.94^\circ$).
