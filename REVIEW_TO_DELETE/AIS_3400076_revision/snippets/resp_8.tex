All samples of each dataset were pooled and randomly permuted with a fixed seed before the 85/10/5 division; the split was not blocked by session or by configuration neighborhood. Because each commanded configuration of the multi-section arm was held for about 3~s and recorded at 1~Hz, some test samples share their configuration with training samples. To quantify the effect, we evaluated the test error with and without these samples: for the multi-section arm, the tip position errors become 4.60, 14.72, and 27.99~mm instead of 4.44, 14.01, and 26.45~mm (Table~S14), and for the single-section arm, samples close to a training configuration are not more accurate than the others (4.08 vs.\ 3.86~mm). Session-blocked evaluation is listed as a limitation. The control targets were selected among the sampled workspace configurations and were therefore not excluded from training; the closed-loop experiments use new camera images, and the trajectories are not part of the datasets. The single-section data were collected in one day and the multi-section data on two days, with the same camera settings and one build of each arm \textbf{[CONFIRM]}; the two arms use different coverings (corrugated silicone and stretchable fabric, Section~2.1).
