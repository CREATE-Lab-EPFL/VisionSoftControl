The large standard deviations are caused by 10 of the 500 test samples (2\%): nine of them are three configurations recorded three times each, whose motion-capture orientation labels imply section bending angles of $87^\circ$--$174^\circ$, far outside the sampled range, which is consistent with orientation flips of the tracked rigid bodies. Excluding these samples, the Section~II relative position error is $9.22 \pm 4.68$~mm instead of $11.92 \pm 25.10$~mm. We discuss these outliers in SI Section~6.1 and report both summaries.
